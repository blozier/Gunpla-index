## Build Notes

First completed GQ kit. Snap fit was clean; most of the time went into airbrushing rather than assembly.

**Frame & fit** — Almost no seam work. A couple of undergated nub gates nearly vanished after a light sand.

**Paint** — Sprayed through the Iwata Eclipse HP-CS, Mr. Color thinned ~2:1 with leveling thinner at ~15 psi. White over a light-gray base; yellow vents masked last.

**Gotchas** — Thruster cans are press-fit and pop loose; a dot of glue fixed it. Finished with Mr. Premium Topcoat Flat.
